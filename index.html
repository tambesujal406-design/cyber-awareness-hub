Four scams you will meet

Different delivery, same goal: get you to act before you think.

Phishing email

A fake message from your bank, courier or employer with a link to a look-alike login page.

Open the official app instead of the link.

SMS scams

Texts that say your KYC expired, your account is blocked or a parcel needs a small fee.

Real banks never ask for details over SMS.

Fake support calls

A caller pretends to be your bank or the police and pushes you to share an OTP or install a screen-sharing app.

Hang up and call the number on your card.

QR code traps

A “scan to receive money” code. Scanning a QR code only ever sends money out.

Never scan to get paid.

Habits that stop most scams

Small routines beat clever tricks.

Pause on urgency

“Act now” and “last warning” are pressure tactics. Real banks give you time.

Never share OTP or PIN

Nobody honest needs your OTP, UPI PIN or card PIN, not even bank staff.

Check the web address

Look for odd spellings and extra words. When in doubt, open the official app yourself.

No PIN to receive money

If a stranger asks you to enter a PIN to get paid, it’s a scam.

Turn on two-step login

Even a stolen password is useless without your second code.

Already clicked or paid?

Move fast. Every minute counts.

  2. Change your passwords, starting with email and banking.
